# DESIGN.md — Arsh Satsangi Engineering Portfolio

## Visual World: Obsidian Electroluminescence
A tactile, high-precision engineering surface inspired by high-end FPGA development suites, laboratory digital storage oscilloscopes, and the minimalist cybernetic elegance of Redoyanul Haque's portfolio.

### Palette & Tokens
- `--bg-void`: `#08060a` (Deepest space obsidian)
- `--bg-surface`: `#110d16` (Low-reflection component base)
- `--bg-surface-hover`: `#181220` (Active trace glow)
- `--accent-lavender`: `#c2a4ff` (Primary electro-violet luminescent signal)
- `--accent-violet`: `#8b5cf6` (Deep harmonic resonance)
- `--signal-emerald`: `#00f5a0` (FPGA clock locked / testbench passed)
- `--signal-amber`: `#ffb84d` (Transient alert / warning state)
- `--text-primary`: `#f5f2f8` (Crisp readability, 15:1 contrast)
- `--text-secondary`: `#b8b0c4` (Tinted off-white, 6.2:1 contrast)
- `--text-muted`: `#8a8096` (Technical annotations, 4.8:1 contrast)
- `--border-subtle`: `rgba(194, 164, 255, 0.12)`
- `--border-focus`: `rgba(194, 164, 255, 0.40)`

### Typography
- **Headings & Narrative**: Geist, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", sans-serif. Balanced scale, tight tracking (-0.02em to -0.03em on display sizes), no empty eyebrows or kickers.
- **Data & Micro-architecture**: JetBrains Mono, SF Mono, Menlo, monospace for register fields, Q-format numbers, timing specs, and Verilog tokens.

### Interaction & Motion Principles
1. **Interactive Entry Matrix**: Minimalist cinematic entry with diagnostics telemetry and a spotlight cursor trigger.
2. **Radial Pointer Spotlight**: Hardware cards react to cursor proximity with a soft optical gradient, creating physical presence.
3. **Hardware Playgrounds**:
   - Live 32-bit CORDIC arithmetic pipeline engine.
   - Live digital oscilloscope visualizing WireGuard line resonance and fault detection.
4. **Web Audio Micro-feedback**: Subtle synthesized 1.2kHz / 800Hz laboratory acoustic bleeps on interactive state triggers (user toggleable).
